Accounting · Human capital · Labor markets

Hi, I’m Martin.

I study how financial reporting and corporate disclosures shape labor markets and investment decisions.

I’m an Assistant Professor in the Department of Accounting at Copenhagen Business School. My research focuses on human capital and employee-related reporting, exploring how information about firms and their workforces matters for employees, investors, and lenders.

I also teach financial accounting and financial statement analysis, and supervise student theses.

Job-market paper

Capitalizing the firm's human capital — an empirical analysis

Employees are often described as a firm’s most valuable asset. What do financial statements miss when they tell us so little about the workforce?

I examine how firms’ reliance on human capital affects the usefulness of financial reporting for assessing risk, and whether workforce information improves bankruptcy prediction.
